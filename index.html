01 / WHO WE ARE

JEENISo

The Junior Entreprise ENISo is a non-profit student consulting organization based at the École Nationale d'Ingénieurs de Sousse.

Founded in 2011, JEENISo gives engineering students the opportunity to apply their theoretical knowledge through real projects for real clients.

The mission

Turn academic knowledge into practical professional experience.

  • Real client projects
  • Technical skills
  • Soft skills development
  • Teamwork & leadership
  • Professional experience
